Privacy Policy — Chart Island Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Chart Island Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Chart Island YouTube channel. It is used solely to manage that one channel.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's authorization, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and caption files, and to set titles, descriptions and publish times on the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or store data about any other user.

How data is stored

The OAuth credentials are stored securely by the owner and are used only to act on the owner's own channel. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not store any YouTube user data other than the IDs of videos the owner uploaded. OAuth credentials are kept only as long as the owner uses the app. The owner can delete all stored credentials at any time, and you can revoke the app's access at Google security settings, after which no further data is accessed. Any stored data is deleted within 30 days of revocation or on request.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner through the Chart Island YouTube channel.
